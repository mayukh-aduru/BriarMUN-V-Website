About Model UN

Diplomacy is a skill. We build it in committee.

Model United Nations puts students in the role of a country delegate, tasked with researching real global issues, negotiating with rival blocs, and drafting resolutions under formal parliamentary procedure — all before the gavel falls.

01

Leadership

Delegates learn to command a room, build coalitions, and steer committee toward resolution.

02

Diplomacy

Negotiation without hostility — managing competing interests to reach real consensus.

03

Public Policy

Delegates study real policy positions and translate them into working resolutions.

04

Research

Every position paper starts with deep research into a country's history and stance.
