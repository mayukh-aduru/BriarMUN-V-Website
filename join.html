Join Briar Woods MUN

No experience, no application, no tryout. Just show up to a Tuesday meeting — here's what to expect.

01 · Meetings

When we meet

Tuesdays, 3:15–4:30 PM in Room 214. Drop in any week — no RSVP needed for your first visit.

02 · Who can join

Open to every grade

Freshmen through seniors are welcome, whether this is your first club or your fourth year of MUN.

03 · What to bring

Just yourself

A notebook helps, but the only real requirement is curiosity about how the world works.

Interest Form

Tell us you're coming

Fill this out and we'll add you to the delegate email list with meeting reminders and conference sign-ups. You can also just show up — this is optional but helps us plan.
