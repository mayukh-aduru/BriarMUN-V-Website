The committee lineup for this year's Falcon Invitational — from General Assembly to specialized crisis rooms. Update this list once your conference topics are finalized.

Crisis & Specialized

Fast-paced, high-stakes rooms

Smaller committees with evolving scenarios, built for experienced delegates who think fast and write faster.

JCC

Joint Crisis Committee: 1962

Advanced

Two linked cabinets navigate a fictionalized Cold War standoff in real time.

IP

International Press Corps

Intermediate

Delegates act as journalists covering the conference — writing, photographing, and publishing live.

SC

UN Security Council

Advanced

The five permanent members and rotating seats respond to a breaking international crisis.
